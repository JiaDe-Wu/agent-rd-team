# Margin and Pricing Rules

Written by Finance. Applies to everyday pricing and to every promotion in the official store. Rule IDs are MGN-n.

| ID | Rule | Why | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| MGN-1 | The **final price** is regular price × discount, rounded to the nearest dollar. The discount is stored as a percent of the regular price (85 means 15% off) and is a whole number from 50 to 100. | One calculation across the company, so numbers reconcile | $599 at 80 gives $479; $599 at 85 gives $509 (509.15 rounds down) |
| MGN-2 | **Margin floor**: for each item, the lowest price during the sale must not be below 108% of cost. For an item in spend-and-save, the lowest price is "final price × 90%" (rounded to the nearest dollar), because the most a shopper can save on the cart is 10%; for an item outside spend-and-save it is the final price. | Gear margins are thin and spend-and-save stacked on a discount is where money is lost | Tent: cost $410, floor $442.80. At 80, final $479, lowest $431 (below the floor): the sale cannot launch. At 90, final $539, lowest $485: passes |
| MGN-3 | The pre-launch check gives the **expected margin rate** of the sale: from the units sold per item in the 30 days before the sale, the margin at regular prices and at final prices (percent, one decimal place). | Whoever approves the sale sees how much margin it gives away | For the submitted plan: 31.3% at regular prices, 17.2% at sale prices; the check shows both numbers |
