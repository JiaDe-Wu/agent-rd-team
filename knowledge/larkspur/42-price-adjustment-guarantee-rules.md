# Price-Adjustment Guarantee Rules

Written by Finance and Customer Success. Rule IDs are PP-n.

| ID | Rule | Why | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| PP-1 | An item marked with the **14-day price-adjustment guarantee** (`priceProtect` is true in the product data): a shopper who bought it in the 14 days before the sale starts (the 14 days ending the day before the start, both ends included) on an order that completed (returned orders do not count) and paid more than the sale's final price gets **the difference times the quantity back**. The comparison is the per-item final price; spend-and-save and coupons are not counted. | Earlier shoppers are not punished for buying early; it is the brand's promise | Paid $599, sale final $479, quantity 1: refund $120 |
| PP-2 | Before launch the check works out the **expected payouts**: how many orders, how much in total. **If the total is more than $20,000 the CFO has to approve the sale before it launches**; with no approval the system cannot launch it. | A price cut also triggers a pile of refunds on earlier orders | Total $23,638: blocked until the CFO approves, or until the discounts change |
| PP-3 | Payouts go to the original payment method automatically within 7 days after the sale ends; the shopper does not have to ask. | Do not make people chase it | |
